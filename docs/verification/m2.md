## Conversion gate with the production rasterizer

`rasterizerUnderTest()` in `VerifyConversion.kt` now returns
`ScanlineRasterizer(SUPERSAMPLE)`: the pure Kotlin rasterizer at 384 px, box
filtered to 96 px, the treatment the inkscape reference gets.

| Measure | ScanlineRasterizer | AwtRasterizer, M1 |
|---|---|---|
| Icons | 15,322 | 15,322 |
| Soft IoU below 0.98 | **5** | 5 |
| Pass rate | **0.99967** (gate 0.999) | 0.99967 |
| Min, 0.1st pct, 1st pct | 0.9013, 0.9908, 0.9942 | 0.9018, 0.9898, 0.9919 |
| Median, mean | 0.9969, 0.9970 | 0.9961, 0.9959 |
| Binary IoU at alpha 128 below 0.98, not gated | 158, median 0.9987 | 178, median 0.9979 |
| Rasterize and compare | 17.5 s, 4 workers | 30.1 s |

The same five icons fail, for the reasons M1 recorded: `opacity` on cino and
fairtiq, cusp cubics on suzuki, royal_road and nekome.

Why 4x supersampling. Run at 96 px directly, the rasterizer failed the gate.
Two runs on uncommitted working states of this task, before the stroker's
arc area correction:

| Variant at 96 px | Below 0.98 | Pass rate | Median |
|---|---|---|---|
| All strokes of an icon in one union pass | 143 | 0.99067 | 0.9912 |
| One pass per primitive, source-over | 604 | 0.96058 | 0.9903 |

The geometry was not the cause. On the same working state, a synthetic ring of radius 10 and width 1
covered 251.20 px against 251.40 for java.awt and 251.33 exact; a sloped
line, 142.48 against 142.58 and 142.67 exact. The cause is stacked
primitives. gc_wizard draws every path twice. An SVG renderer composites each
pass source-over, which darkens shared edge pixels, and by an amount that
depends on pixel size. The reference does that at 384 px. Matching its pixel
size isolates the geometry, which is what the gate measures. The renderer
keeps source-over per primitive, SVG's semantics, at the output size.

## Goldens

`render/src/test/resources/golden/<mode>/<icon>.png`, 432 px, 96 files,
2.7 MB. Each mode has its own theme so the goldens also cover every
background shape but square, both gradients, borders, miter joins and square
caps. Square and disabled backgrounds are covered in `BackgroundTest` and
`CompositorTest`. The 24 icons:

fdroid, camera, google_chrome, firefox, signal, telegram, whatsapp,
google_maps, youtube, spotify, calculator, clock, contacts, gallery,
messages, phone, settings, weather, notes, termux, vlc, wikipedia,
keepassdx, newpipe.

The comparison is of the PNG file bytes, since the encoder is deterministic
(changed in the final review; it decoded and compared pixels before). A
golden made on the JVM must match the phone bit for bit, because nothing in
the pixel path depends on the platform.

## Lone lines

Invert fill goldens, `golden/invert/`, white is the line paint and teal the
body:

- `fdroid.png`: both antennas are unenclosed and drawn as lines. They stop
  short of the head, by the gap.
- `camera.png`: the small dot is a gap ring with a filled centre, set into
  the body fill.
- `google_chrome.png`: the three inner lines are gaps with fill on both
  sides, out to the outer ring.

`GoldenTest.loneLinesAreHandledOnFdroidCameraAndChrome` probes those points
in the rendered image. Checked against the broken case: with keep lone lines
off, it fails on F-Droid's antenna, `rgb(23, 48, 44)`, background instead of
line.

## Timings, JVM

`./gradlew :render:benchmarkRenderer`. OpenJDK 21.0.12.1, 24 CPUs. Icons
spread evenly over the bundle, default theme in each fill mode, cold mask
cache, best of 3 after a warm-up.

| Mode | 24 icons at 128 px | 300 at 432 px, render | 300 at 432 px, render and PNG | Same, 4 threads | PNG size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Outline | 7 ms | 460 ms | 2,984 ms | 805 ms | 15 KB |
| Invert fill | 35 ms | 3,748 ms | 6,023 ms | 1,612 ms | 13 KB |
| Silhouette | 22 ms | 2,558 ms | 4,975 ms | 1,299 ms | 10 KB |
| Outline + Silhouette | 22 ms | 2,583 ms | 4,891 ms | 1,321 ms | 10 KB |

A paint change on a warm cache re-renders the 24 previews in 2 ms.

The phone budgets in spec 3.3, 150 ms for the preview and 20 s for Apply,
are verified on the emulator in Task 4.

The deflate settings trade size for speed. On 40 icons, an Outline theme and
an Invert theme with a diagonal gradient: chains of 4,096 gave 15 and 19 KB
per icon at 29.7 and 67.8 ms; chains of 512 with a nice length of 64, the
setting kept, gave 15 and 25 KB at 9.8 and 17.7 ms. Against Python's zlib
at level 9 on the same filtered data, the golden IDAT totals per mode are:
Outline 363 vs 365 KB, Invert 587 vs 443, Silhouette 989 vs 875, Outline +
Silhouette 580 vs 566. The gap is the diagonal gradient on the Invert theme.
