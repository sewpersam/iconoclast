## Task 6: the theme editor

### Renderer fixes

- `FillKey` split. The fill key now holds the mode, plus gap and closing only where the
  mode reads them. Invert fill caches its body before the fallback test, and its lone
  lines under `LoneLinesKey`. Threshold and lone-lines changes hit the cache:
  `CompositorTest.thresholdAndLoneLinesReuseTheFillMasks`. Outline no longer counts the
  geometry bytes twice.
- Exterior pockets. A pocket that neither grown side reaches now stays outside when it
  borders only the grown outside. `FillModesTest.exteriorPocketsStayOutsideWhenClosingExceedsGap`
  failed on the old code ("channel at y 20 expected 0.0 but was 1.0") and passes now.
- New goldens: `pockets/signal.png` and `pockets/fdroid.png`, invert fill at gap 0.25,
  closing 2. They equal the fixed render and differ from the old one. Old: signal's dashed
  ring filled solid between dashes, fdroid's head-to-body slot filled.
- Goldens changed by the fix: 10 `silhouette` and 11 `outline_silhouette` icons, 1 to 76
  pixels each. Every changed pixel is webbing in an acute exterior notch, as at the
  serifs and crossings of wikipedia's W. Same defect, closing 0.5 sealing notches

### Preview strategy

- Previews re-rasterise at 128 px. They never downscale the 432 px output: that costs about
  9 times the pixels per icon and would break the 150 ms budget.
- Parity: the only decision that differs by resolution is invert fill's fallback.
  `PreviewParityTest` checks it at 128 and 432 px for 24 samples under 4 golden themes and
  2 presets. Result: `fallback parity: 144 checked, 0 differ`.
- 128 px also while dragging, per the Task 5 ruling. Instead of a lower resolution:
  - One pass in flight. Requests made meanwhile overwrite each other; only the newest runs
    next. Nothing queues, nothing is cancelled mid-pass.
  - During a drag a pass draws only the visible cells. Release draws the rest.
  - Four render threads. Bitmaps cached per (theme, drawable, size), 512 entries.
- App start runs a render warm-up: a synthetic icon in all four fill modes on a throwaway
  renderer.

### Gate, icondesign36

Method:

- FPS: `adb shell input swipe` over the stroke-weight slider for 3000 ms.
  `dumpsys gfxinfo dev.iconoclast.app framestats` dumped at 1.6 s and after release,
  merged by IntendedVsync. FPS = frames over first IntendedVsync to last
  SwapBuffersCompleted. FrameCompleted is not used: under `-gpu host` it carries a stale
  GPU fence time.
- Preview time: the view model logs every pass to tag `IconoclastEditor`, e.g.
  `preview pass: 24 drawn, 0 cached, 24 shown at 128 px in 63 ms`.
- Theme: Jade, invert fill, 24 samples. Debug build.
- Cold: `am kill` in the background, then reopen. The editor restores into Samples, so the
  first pass in the new process draws all 24 with a cold mask cache, cold bitmap cache and
  cold JIT.
- Warm: a full 24-icon pass after a geometry change with the JIT warm. Every mask is new,
  nothing is cached.

| Measure | Budget | Result | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| FPS, 3 s stroke-weight drag | 30 fps | 60.0 fps, 174 frames in 2.90 s. Vsync interval p50 16.7, p99 16.7, max 16.7 ms. 42 preview passes during the drag, mean 71 ms, max 82 ms | PASS |
| Same drag, before the warm-up build | 30 fps | 58.1 and 59.1 fps | PASS |
| Warm, 24 icons at 128 px | 150 ms | 48 and 61 ms after a cap change. Release passes after drags: 44 to 63 ms | PASS |
| Cold, 24 icons at 128 px, with warm-up | 150 ms | 216, 227, 212 ms | FAIL |
| Cold, before the warm-up | 150 ms | 387, 332, 371 ms | FAIL |

The cold miss was given one optimisation pass, the warm-up, and was not tuned further. The
warm-up takes 363 to 412 ms on a background thread. It overlaps the first pass and does
not finish before it. Through home, the usual path, the first editor pass drew 15
installed icons in 180 ms, on the build without the warm-up.
