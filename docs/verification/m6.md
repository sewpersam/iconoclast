### Apply holds no pack in heap (f290b27)

PackBuilder writes each PNG to `cache/pack/icons/` as it is drawn;
ZipRewriter streams those files into `cache/pack/unsigned.apk`; PackSigner
signs file to file. Heap limit on icondesign36: `dalvik.vm.heapgrowthlimit`
192 MB. Peak heap is `Runtime.totalMemory - freeMemory` sampled every 5 ms,
so it includes garbage not yet collected; native heap is
`Debug.getNativeHeapAllocatedSize`.

| Apply, 300 @ 432 px | Cold | Warm runs | Per icon | Pack | Peak Java heap | Peak native |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grain 0.6 (`effect_grain`, `applyOf300IconsGrain`) | 14648 ms | 14545, 14590, 14531 | 295 KB | 91.4 MB | 144 to 151 MB | 5 MB |
| Same look, grain at full amount (1.0, opacity 1) | 14556 ms | | 342 KB | 106.0 MB | 150 MB | 5 MB |
| Same look, no grain | 10125 ms | | 11 KB | 3.9 MB | 136 MB | 5 MB |

Grain at 0.6 adds 283 KB per icon, at full amount 331 KB. The heap peak
with a 91 MB pack is within 15 MB of the 4 MB pack's: the pack is not held.
The old path held 300 PNGs (88 MB), the growing rewrite buffer and its
copy (91 MB each) at once, over the 192 MB limit.

Apply in the app after the change: Neon, 15 apps, `render+rewrite 639 ms,
sign 432 ms, 984620 bytes`, `install: Success`; `cache/pack/` holds only
`pack.apk` afterwards.
