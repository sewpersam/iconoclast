cracks.png is original work made for this project's tests, not derived from any image:
white cell borders of a toroidal Voronoi diagram of 36 seeded random points, 256 x 256,
so it tiles. Generated by a short script (Python random.Random(2026), pixel = clamp((3 - (d2 - d1)) / 2)
where d1 and d2 are the distances to the two nearest points) and encoded by the project's
PNG encoder, so its SHA-256 is c6d24d8999ff28eae18eca0d2869b0d900a6ea577f9727ecbea421a78ffbb395.
